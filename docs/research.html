Research & Experience

Current Research at Case Western Reserve University

Evaluating community–health system care coordination. The Better Health Pathways HUB coordinates community health workers across Northeast Ohio to help clients meet health-related social needs such as housing, food, and transportation. My work combines interviews with community health workers, care coordination agencies, and health system partners with linked HUB and health care utilization data. The goal is to understand what makes this model work and last, and whether completing social-needs pathways is associated with better health outcomes.

CONNECT-Pantries. An implementation science project with the Prevention Research Center for Healthy Neighborhoods. We analyze focus group data to describe how the Ask-Advise-Connect tobacco cessation intervention is adapted for food pantries, guided by the RE-AIM and FRAME-IS frameworks.

Persistent poverty and health. A national analysis in R comparing CDC PLACES health outcomes between persistent-poverty and other census tracts and counties.
